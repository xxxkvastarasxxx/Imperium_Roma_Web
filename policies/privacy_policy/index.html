01 Who we are

Imperium Roma ("we", "us") runs the websites imperiumroma.com and domus.imperiumroma.com, home of Domus, our collection app. We decide how and why the personal data described here is used, which makes us its controller.

Questions about your data go to imperiumroma0@gmail.com or through our contact form.

02 What we collect

When you browse imperiumroma.com

  • Server logs. Like any website, our hosting and network providers record your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and when, for security and to fix problems.
  • Your language. If you switch language, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. We set no cookies and use no analytics or advertising trackers. Our network provider, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary security cookie if it needs to tell visitors from bots.

When you contact us

The contact form sends us your name, email address, the subject, your message and any files you attach. We use them to answer you and, if you ask, to carry out a service such as an authenticity check or market evaluation.

When you subscribe to the newsletter

We keep your email address on our mailing list. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.

When you identify a coin with AI

AI identification on the Services page sends the two photos you choose (obverse and reverse) to Anthropic's Claude AI, which reads the coin. We don't keep the photos. To match the coin against published catalogues we then look up the ruler and denomination on Nomisma.org; nothing about you is part of that lookup.

Tools that run in your browser

The 3D coin viewer and the market valuation tool on the Services page work entirely on your device: images you open in them are not uploaded to us. The 3D viewer loads its graphics library from public content networks (cdnjs and jsDelivr), which see your IP address as any site you load files from would.

When you use Domus

  • Sign-in details: your email address and password, or your Google account if you choose "Continue with Google", in which case Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • Profile: what you add, such as your nickname, name, location, preferred currency and profile picture, plus your plan, your public or private setting and when you were last online.
  • Collection and wishlist: your coins with their photos, legends, references, condition, provenance, prices paid and notes; the 3D models made from your photos; and the coins on your wishlist.
  • Community: your forum threads and replies, the members you follow, and your notifications and notification settings.
  • Plan and payments: which plan you are on. If you buy a paid plan, the payment is handled by Stripe; we receive confirmation of the payment, not your full card details.
  • Security records: sign-in times and IP addresses, used to keep accounts safe.

Some Domus features run on your device too. Removing the background from coin photos happens in your browser, using image models downloaded from img.ly's servers. To show values in your currency, Domus fetches exchange rates from public services (ExchangeRate-API and Frankfurter); those requests contain currency codes only.

03 Why we use it

We only use personal data where the law gives us a reason to. For each purpose:

Purpose Data Legal basis
Running your Domus account and its features Account, profile, collection and community data Contract: needed to provide the service you signed up for
Answering messages and carrying out services you request Contact form details and attachments Contract, or our legitimate interest in replying to you
Sending the newsletter Email address Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time
Identifying a coin with AI The two photos you upload Contract: needed to give you the result you asked for
Keeping the site and community safe: stopping spam, abuse and fraud, and moderating the forum IP addresses, logs, account and forum records Our legitimate interest in a safe, working service
Meeting legal obligations, such as keeping tax records for paid plans What the law requires Legal obligation

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

04 What other members see

In Domus, you decide what others see:

  • With a private profile, only you can see your coins, and nobody sees when you were last online.
  • With a public profile, other members can see your profile, the coins you haven't hidden from it, and when you were last online. You can hide individual coins at any time.
  • Forum posts are visible to other members, shown with your nickname and profile picture.
  • Members can search for each other by nickname and follow one another.

You can change these choices at any time in your Domus profile and settings.

05 How long we keep it

  • Domus account: for as long as you keep it. When you delete your account, it is permanently erased after a 14-day recovery period, and copies in backups are overwritten within 90 days.
  • Contact messages: as long as we need to deal with your request and any follow-up. Ask us and we'll delete them sooner.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • AI identification photos: we don't store them. Anthropic processes them to return the result and keeps them only for a limited period under its own policy.
  • Anti-abuse limits: a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, for up to 24 hours.
  • Server logs: for the limited period set by our hosting and network providers.

06 Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See the personal data we hold about you and get a copy
  • Correct anything that's wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your data
  • Take it with you in a machine-readable format
  • Object to or restrict how we use it
  • Withdraw consent you've given, such as for the newsletter

Most of this you can do yourself in Domus: edit your profile and coins, export your collection, and delete your account (see Delete Your Data). For anything else, email imperiumroma0@gmail.com. We reply within one month and may first ask you to confirm your identity.

California residents also have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to a data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK or the authority in your EU country. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

07 International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. When data from the UK or EU leaves those areas, we rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as adequacy decisions and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

08 Security

All traffic to imperiumroma.com and domus.imperiumroma.com is encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, access to our systems is limited to the people who need it, and the contact form and AI tool are rate-limited against abuse. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

09 Children

Imperium Roma and Domus are for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10 Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we'll tell Domus users by email or in the app before it takes effect.

11 Contact us

Email imperiumroma0@gmail.com or use the contact form. We aim to reply within 5–7 working days.
