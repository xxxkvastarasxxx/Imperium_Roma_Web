Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: February 13, 2026

Your Right to Deletion

Under applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Article 17 ("Right to Erasure"), UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to request deletion of your personal information that we have collected and stored.

This page explains how to submit a deletion request, what data will be deleted, and important information about the deletion process.

1. What Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, we will permanently delete or anonymize the following personal information:

1.1 Account Information

  • Your username, email address, and authentication credentials
  • Profile information and account settings
  • Login history and session data

1.2 User-Generated Content

  • Coin collection data (items, descriptions, notes)
  • Wishlist entries and saved searches
  • Uploaded images and photos
  • Comments, reviews, and forum posts (if applicable)
  • Authentication and valuation requests

1.3 Technical and Usage Data

  • IP addresses and device identifiers linked to your account
  • Cookies and tracking data associated with your account
  • Activity logs and usage analytics tied to your identity

2. Exceptions and Limitations

We may retain certain information when we have a legal obligation or legitimate reason to do so:

2.1 Legal and Regulatory Requirements

  • Information required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal processes
  • Records necessary for tax, accounting, or audit purposes
  • Data subject to legal holds, court orders, or active investigations

2.2 Fraud Prevention and Security

  • Information necessary to detect, prevent, or investigate fraud, security breaches, or abuse
  • Records of accounts terminated for violations of our Terms of Use

2.3 Contractual Obligations

  • Transaction records for completed auctions or sales
  • Communication records related to disputes or customer service issues

2.4 Anonymized Data

  • Aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you may be retained for analytics and research purposes

2.5 Backup Systems

Deleted data may persist in backup systems for up to 90 days before being permanently purged. During this period, the data will not be accessible through our Service or used for any purpose.

3. How to Request Deletion

You can submit a deletion request through the following methods:

3.1 Submission Methods

  • Email: Send your request to imperiumroma0@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  • Contact Form: Submit a request via https://imperiumroma.com/contact/
  • Account Settings: Use the "Delete Account" option in your account settings (if available)

3.2 Required Information

To process your request efficiently and verify your identity, please include:

  • Your full name associated with the account
  • The email address registered to your account
  • Your username (if applicable)
  • Any additional information that helps us locate your account
  • Confirmation that you wish to permanently delete all account data

3.3 Identity Verification

For security and privacy protection, we may ask you to verify your identity before processing your deletion request. This may involve:

  • Logging into your account
  • Responding to an email sent to your registered email address
  • Providing additional identifying information

We will not process deletion requests from unverified sources to prevent unauthorized deletion of accounts.

4. Processing Timeline

  • Acknowledgment: We will acknowledge receipt of your request within 5 business days
  • Verification: Identity verification (if required) may take 3-7 business days
  • Processing: We aim to complete your deletion request within 30 days of verification (as required by GDPR)
  • Confirmation: You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete

Note: In some cases, particularly those involving complex legal or technical issues, the timeline may be extended up to 60 days. We will notify you if an extension is necessary.

5. Consequences of Deletion

Before requesting deletion, please be aware of the following consequences:

5.1 Permanent and Irreversible

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted:

  • You will lose access to all features requiring authentication
  • Your collection, wishlist, and saved data will be permanently lost
  • You will be unable to recover any User Content, images, or notes
  • You will not be able to reactivate your account or restore deleted data

5.2 Outstanding Obligations

If you have any outstanding transactions, auctions, or disputes, those must be resolved before account deletion can be completed.

5.3 Future Use

After deletion, if you wish to use our Service again, you will need to create a new account. Your previous data will not be associated with the new account.

6. Alternatives to Full Deletion

If you're not ready for permanent deletion, consider these alternatives:

  • Account Deactivation: Temporarily disable your account without deleting data (contact us for options)
  • Data Correction: Update or correct inaccurate personal information in your account settings
  • Data Export: Request a copy of your data in a portable format before deletion
  • Selective Deletion: Delete specific collections, images, or content without closing your account
  • Marketing Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from newsletters and marketing communications while keeping your account active

7. Additional Privacy Rights

In addition to deletion, you have other rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, please see our Privacy Policy or contact us using the information below.

8. Questions and Concerns

If you have questions about the deletion process or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us:

Email: imperiumroma0@gmail.com
Contact Form: https://imperiumroma.com/contact/
Response Time: We aim to respond within 5-7 business days

8.1 Lodge a Complaint

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your deletion request or believe we have not complied with applicable data protection laws, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority:

  • EU/EEA: Contact your national Data Protection Authority (DPA)
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) - https://ico.org.uk/
  • California: California Attorney General's Office - https://oag.ca.gov/
