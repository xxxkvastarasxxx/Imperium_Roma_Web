Delete Your Data

How to delete your Domus account, what happens to your data, and how to have anything else we hold about you removed.

The short version

  • Delete your account yourself, in Domus settings.
  • You have 14 days to change your mind.
  • After that, your account and collection are erased for good.
  • No Domus account? Email us and we'll remove what we hold.

01 Delete your Domus account

The quickest way is to do it yourself:

  1. Open Settings

    Sign in to Domus and go to Settings.

  2. Choose to delete your account

    We email you a confirmation link. Your account stays fully active until you open it.

  3. Confirm from the email

    A 14-day recovery period starts. Your account is restricted during it; to cancel, sign in and choose "Restore my account".

  4. Deleted for good

    After 14 days your account, profile and collection are permanently erased.

Open Domus settings

Before you go: want a copy of your collection? Export it from Domus first, or email us and we'll send it to you. If you have a paid plan, cancel it on the Subscription page so you aren't charged again.

02 What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, sign-in details and any link to your Google account
  • Your profile: nickname, name, profile picture and settings
  • Your collection and wishlist: coins, photos, notes, provenance, prices and 3D models
  • Your activity: the members you follow, your notifications and notification settings

03 What we may keep

A few things can outlast your account, and only for as long as needed:

  • Backups: copies are overwritten within 90 days and can't be reached through the service in the meantime.
  • Legal records: what the law requires us to keep, such as payment and tax records for paid plans.
  • Safety records: what we need to handle a dispute or prevent abuse, such as a record that an account was banned.
  • Anonymous statistics that can't identify you.

04 Other data we hold

  • Newsletter: use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter, or email us, and your address is removed from our list.
  • Messages you sent us: email us and we'll delete your contact-form messages and any files you attached.
  • AI identification photos: nothing to delete. We don't store them.
  • Server logs expire automatically.

05 Ask us by email

If you can't sign in, don't have a Domus account, or want something specific removed, email imperiumroma0@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Please:

  • write from the email address linked to your account, or the one you used to contact us
  • include your Domus nickname, if you have one
  • say what you'd like deleted: everything, or something specific

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm it's you before we delete anything. We'll let you know when it's done, within one month of your request.

06 Your other rights

You can also ask to see, correct or get a copy of your data, or object to how we use it. The Privacy Policy explains how.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your request, you can complain to a data protection authority, for example:

  • EU: the data protection authority in your country
  • UK: the Information Commissioner's Office
  • California: the California Attorney General
