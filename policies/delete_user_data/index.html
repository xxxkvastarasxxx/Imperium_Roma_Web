Effective Date: April 8, 2025

How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. Submit your request through one of the following methods:

  • Email: privacy@imperiumroma.com
  • Web Form: https://imperiumroma.com/contact/

For security, we may ask you to verify your identity before completing your request.

What Will Be Deleted

We will delete your account profile, collection entries, wishlist items, comments, and any other personal data we store, except where retention is required by law.

Processing Time

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days.
