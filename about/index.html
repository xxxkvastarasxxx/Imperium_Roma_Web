Our Story

Our Story

From a shared frustration to a global platform — built by collectors, for collectors

2022

The Spark

A group of collectors shared one frustration — no single, reliable place to authenticate, value, and study ancient Roman coins. Scattered catalogues, locked databases, incomparable pricing. We decided to make difference

2023

First Research Tools

A shared spreadsheet of auction results grew into our first cross-referenced database — linking live market data with a forgery archive built on die-link analysis and provenance research

2024

Public Launch

We opened to fellow enthusiasts and the response proved we were solving a real problem — collectors across Europe began submitting coins for authentication, tracking prices, and building digital catalogues

2025

Domus & AI

We launched Domus — a free collection-management dashboard — alongside AI-assisted coin identification, real-time market valuation, and 3D coin modelling from user photographs

Today

10 + Countries & Growing

Seven specialists in authentication, valuation, restoration, and research now serve collectors, dealers, and scholars worldwide — because trust is the foundation of numismatics
